    How to run this

    Students go to 15010.ai/l1-a, with your own section letter in place of the a. This page is the same address with xx on the end: 15010.ai/l1-axx. Capitals work too; they land in the same place.

    On the day

    1. Press Go live first. Until you do, a student who submits is told the session has not started. Nothing breaks, but nothing is recorded either, so do this before the link goes on screen.
    2. Put the student address up. Answers appear on the chart as they arrive, newest first, and the panel on the right keeps the count.
    3. Talk about round 1 while it fills. Show the numbers opens the ranked list if you want names and figures.
    4. Start round 2 when you are ready. Every phone changes question at the same moment; round 1 stays on the chart.
    5. Both puts the two curves up together. Hovering a student now lights the same person on the other curve, which is the comparison the second round exists to make.
    6. Surplus, then type a price, then Build consumer surplus to add the class up one student at a time.

    Which curve

    Round 1 · Round 2 · Both
    Which demand curve is drawn. The numbers on the right always describe one round only: averaging across two different conditions would mean nothing. With Both up they follow the later round.

    What the chart shows

    Students
    One dot per answer. Hover anywhere in a student's column to pick them out: guides drop to both axes and a label names them. With both curves up, their answer from the other round lights up at the same time.
    Price
    Hovering draws a price line across the chart and reads off how many students would buy at it. Click to lock the line so it stays put while you talk, and click again to release it.
    Surplus
    Opens the price box. Hovering a student then shows what they personally gain: the gap between what they would have paid and what they actually pay. Below the price they simply do not buy, and get nothing, not a negative amount, which is the mistake worth heading off out loud.
    Price $
    The price everyone pays, in surplus mode. Type it and the line is fixed there; unlike the hovering line in Price mode it does not follow the cursor.
    Build consumer surplus
    Adds the buyers up one at a time, highest first. The first three land slowly and named, so the room sees that a bar is one person and what they gained; after that it speeds up, but every student still gets their own bar, because the area is what they add up to. Press again to stop. With both curves up they build together, so the class watches one area outgrow the other.

    Live submissions

    Go live · Stop
    Connects this page to the room. Students cannot submit until it has been pressed once. Stop disconnects the board; it does not close the poll, and answers already given are kept.
    Start round 2
    Moves the whole class to the second condition at once. Every phone swaps to the new question and clears its box; round 1 answers stay on the chart and in the data.
    Start over
    Wipes every answer in the room and returns to round 1, on every phone as well as here. Two clicks, not a dialog. Use it between sections, or after a rehearsal. Not to fix one bad answer, since a student can simply resubmit and their new answer replaces the old one.

    Show the numbers

    The list, and High to low · Low to high
    Every answer, ranked. Hovering a row lights that student on the chart and vice versa. The rank is always by willingness to pay, so a student keeps the same number whichever way you sort.

    Simulate a class

    Students arrive · Add one · Undo · Clear all
    Invented answers, for rehearsing on your own. They are folded away at the bottom of the page because a visible "Students arrive" is one mis-click from burying a real class in fake data. Press Clear all before the room fills. Going live also drops any simulated answers for the round you are on.
