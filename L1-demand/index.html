    How to run this

    On the day

    Students go to 15010.ai/l1-a
    You are on 15010.ai/l1-axx
    Swap in your own section letter. Capitals work too.

    1. Press Go live before anything else. Until you do, students who try to answer are told the session has not started.
    2. Put the address on screen. Answers appear on the chart as they arrive.
    3. Talk while it fills, and open Show the numbers if you want names and figures.
    4. Press Start round 2 when you are ready. Every phone changes at once, and round 1 stays on the chart.
    5. Switch to Both, then hover a student to see the same person under both conditions.
    6. Switch to Surplus, set a price, and press Build consumer surplus.

    Which curve is drawn

    Round 1Round 2Both
    Chooses which curve is on the chart. The numbers on the right always describe a single round, since averaging across two different conditions would not mean anything; with Both up they follow the later round.

    What the chart shows

    Students
    One dot per answer. Hover over a student to see their name and read their number off both axes. With both curves up, their answer from the other round lights up at the same time.
    Price
    Hovering draws a price line across the chart and tells you how many students would buy at it. Click to lock the line in place while you talk about it.
    Surplus
    Set a price, then hover over a student to see what they gain: their maximum, less what they actually pay. Below the price they simply do not buy, so they gain nothing rather than something negative.
    Price $
    The price everyone pays. Once you type it the line stays put, unlike the one that follows your cursor in Price mode.
    Build consumer surplus
    Adds the buyers up one at a time, highest first. The first three arrive slowly and named, so the room sees that each bar is one person, and after that it speeds up. Press again to stop.

    Live submissions

    Go liveStop
    Opens the room for answers. Students cannot submit until you have pressed it once. Stop simply disconnects this board and leaves everything already submitted in place.
    Start round 2
    Moves the whole class to the second question at once. Round 1 answers stay on the chart.
    Start over
    Clears every answer and returns to round 1, on the phones as well as here, and takes two clicks to confirm. Use it between sections rather than to fix a single answer, since a student can simply submit again.

    Show the numbers

    High to lowLow to high
    Every answer, ranked. Hovering a row lights that student on the chart, and hovering the chart lights their row.
