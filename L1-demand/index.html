    How to run this

    On the day

    Students go to 15010.ai/l1-a
    You are on 15010.ai/l1-axx
    Swap in your own section letter. Capitals work.

    1. Go live first. Until you do, students are told the session has not started.
    2. Put the address up. Answers land on the chart as they arrive.
    3. Talk while it fills. Show the numbers if you want names and figures.
    4. Start round 2. Every phone changes at once. Round 1 stays up.
    5. Both, then hover a student to see the same person under both conditions.
    6. Surplus, set a price, Build consumer surplus.

    Which curve is drawn

    Round 1Round 2Both
    The numbers on the right describe one round only, the later one when Both is up. Averaging across two conditions would mean nothing.

    What the chart shows

    Students
    One dot per answer. Hover to name a student and read their number off both axes. With both curves up, their other answer lights up too.
    Price
    Hover for a price line and how many would buy at it. Click to lock it while you talk.
    Surplus
    Hover a student to see what they gain: their maximum, less the price. Below the price they do not buy, so they get nothing. Not a negative.
    Price $
    The price everyone pays. Fixed once typed, unlike the line that follows the cursor in Price mode.
    Build consumer surplus
    Adds the buyers up one at a time, highest first. The first three are slow and named, so the room sees that a bar is one person; then it speeds up. Press again to stop.

    Live submissions

    Go liveStop
    Opens the room. Students cannot submit until you press it. Stop only disconnects the board; answers are kept.
    Start round 2
    Switches every phone to the second question at once. Round 1 stays on the chart.
    Start over
    Wipes the room back to round 1, phones included. Two clicks to confirm. For between sections, not for fixing one answer: a student can just resubmit.

    Show the numbers

    High to lowLow to high
    Every answer, ranked. Hover a row to light that student on the chart, or the chart to light their row.
