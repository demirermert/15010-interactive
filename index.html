📚 How to Use This Tool

  1. Enter Demand Formula: Input the inverse demand equation where P is price and Q is quantity (e.g., "120 - 2*Q")
  2. Enter Supply Formula: Input the inverse supply equation (e.g., "2*Q")
  3. Set Tariff Amount: Enter the tariff in thousands of dollars (e.g., 4 for $4,000)
  4. Calculate: Click "Calculate & Visualize" to see the results
  5. Analyze: The graph will show:
    • Original demand curve (blue)
    • Original supply curve (red)
    • New supply curve with tariff (orange, shifted up)
    • Equilibrium points before and after tariff

Key Concepts

  • Equilibrium: The point where supply and demand curves intersect
  • Pass-Through Rate: The percentage of the tariff that gets passed on to consumers through higher prices
  • Deadweight Loss: The loss in economic efficiency when the equilibrium is not achieved (the triangle between the old and new equilibrium)
